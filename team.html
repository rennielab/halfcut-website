Team & Board

Who
we are.

A small but mighty crew backing big ideas, and we're in this for the long haul. From grassroots organisers to number-crunchers and policy nerds, every person on this team shares one drive, to protect the Daintree and back the Eastern Kuku Yalanji Bama people to lead the way.

Who actually
does the work.

The rangers, Elders, and corporation staff of Jabalbina Yalanji Aboriginal Corporation are not HalfCut employees. They are the reason HalfCut exists. We publish their work at their invitation, in their words, and with their approval.

Meet the Eastern Kuku Yalanji ranger program on the Projects page, or visit Jabalbina's own site for the fullest picture of their work.
